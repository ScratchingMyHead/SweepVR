Sweep controls

Sweep control activation and actions are decided by where on the control you enter and leave — you never have to dwell on a control. Entry points are marked with notches; usually you leave perpendicular to how you entered, but this varies slightly per control type.

Buttons

  • Enter from the left or right through a notch to arm.
  • Leave through the top or bottom to press.
  • Leave back out the side to cancel — sliding along a row of buttons to reach a far one presses nothing on the way.

Repeat buttons (scroll arrows, zoom)

  • Enter from the side: fires once immediately.
  • Stay on it: after a short pause it keeps firing until you leave.

Dropdowns (e.g. bookmarks)

  • Enter the button from the side: the list opens.
  • Move onto the list: the highlight follows, no dwell.
  • Leave sideways to choose the highlighted row; leave up or down to cancel.

Drags (scrollbar thumb)

  • Enter from either end of its travel: it snaps to you and follows.
  • Leave sideways to drop it there; carry on past either end to abandon the drag unchanged.

Tips

  • If you see text ghosting in the browser adjust the eye separation in settings
